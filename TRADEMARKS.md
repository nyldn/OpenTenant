# OpenTenant trademark policy

The software and documentation licences do not grant rights to the OpenTenant
name, logos, visual identity, domain names, or other source identifiers. This
policy does not assert that any mark is registered.

## Uses that do not require permission

You may:

- make truthful, non-confusing references to the OpenTenant project;
- state that software is compatible with, based on, or derived from
  OpenTenant;
- link to the official repository; and
- retain required copyright, licence, and attribution notices.

These uses must not imply sponsorship, certification, endorsement, or official
status.

## Uses that require written permission

Written permission from the trademark owner is required to:

- use OpenTenant, a confusingly similar name, or an OpenTenant logo as the
  primary name or branding of a product, service, company, organisation,
  package, application, domain, or hosted offering;
- use the branding in advertising or merchandising; or
- claim that a fork, modified distribution, service, training programme, or
  certification is official or endorsed.

Forks and modified distributions should use distinct names and branding while
retaining legally required notices and accurately describing their origin.

The owner must complete a trademark clearance review and name the legal
trademark steward before the repository is made public. Questions and
permission requests will be routed through the official repository once that
steward is named.
