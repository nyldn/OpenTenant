## Scoring method

Each sentence in a rubric row is one equally weighted criterion. A criterion earns 1 when met and 0 when not met. For a compound criterion, independently testable conjuncts share its credit equally; partial credit is shown explicitly. A required execution environment is part of its predicate: a local run cannot establish a CI-timed scaffold or a suite green in CI. Scores are `5 × earned criterion units / listed criterion units`, followed by the R11 cap. Calculations use unrounded fractions until the final total.

Local, CI, publication and production columns describe separate evidence domains. A blank domain earns no evidence credit, but lack of production does not reduce a criterion that only asks for implemented, locally testable behavior. Here, "external audit checkpoint" means outside PostgreSQL, as the implementation and threat model specify. It does not mean a cloud service or independently retained production anchor.
